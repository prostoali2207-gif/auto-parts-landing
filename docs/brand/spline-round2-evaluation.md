# Spline — Brand Refinement Round 2

Status: **CLOSED — CURRENT A1.1 RETAINED**.

Production `public/spline-wordmark.svg` remains unchanged.

## Goal
Try to beat the current A1.1 BALANCED wordmark without redesign-for-redesign's-sake.

A challenger could advance only if it was clearly stronger on immediate SPLINE readability, distinctiveness, memorability, mechanical credibility, 24–32 px survival, monochrome use, non-generic character, and practical deployment.

## Control — A1.1 BALANCED
Strengths: readable, compact, path-based, monochrome, already validated at 24/32/48 px. Its SP intervention is simple enough to survive reduction and reverse use.

## Challenger A2 — Restrained Interlock
Asset: `public/brand-exploration/spline-round2-a-interlock.svg`

A2 replaced the simple rounded SP slot with a restrained keyed negative-space joint. Conceptually it ties more directly to fit / mating / connection.

### Final gate
A2 does **not** beat A1.1 clearly enough to justify replacing production.

Reason: the keyed geometry introduces sub-features that become approximately sub-pixel to ~1 px at normal 24–32 px header rendering. That makes the proprietary step vulnerable to antialiasing, closure and accidental visual noise. The existing A1.1 uses a simpler open slot, so its connection cue remains more stable and predictable at the sizes that matter most.

A2 therefore gains conceptual specificity but loses robustness. For a young automotive-parts brand, that is the wrong trade.

## B — Indexed Precision
Killed. The datum/index treatment behaves more like supporting identity graphics than a stronger primary logo. It does not beat the control on memorability or ownable wordmark construction.

## C — Coupled S
Killed. Moving the intervention into S increases glitch/software/chain-link risk and weakens effortless first-glance reading.

## Final decision
**KEEP A1.1 BALANCED.**

No challenger in Round 2 produced a material improvement large enough to justify a logo change.

This is a positive result: the current wordmark survived a deliberate challenger round rather than being accepted by inertia.

Brand rule remains:

**Read SPLINE first. Notice connection second.**

Future logo exploration should not restart unless a real application exposes a concrete failure of A1.1 (for example signage reproduction, embroidery, a required compact avatar, Arabic co-branding, or trademark/similarity evidence).
