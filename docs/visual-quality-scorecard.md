# Spline — Visual Quality Scorecard

Use this scorecard for rendered visual reviews and benchmark comparisons. Score actual output, not design intent.

## Scale
- 10 — exceptional, reference-quality execution for this project scope.
- 9 — excellent; clearly competitive with strong professional work.
- 8 — strong; polished and commercially credible, with noticeable room to improve.
- 7 — competent; works, but lacks refinement or distinction.
- 6 — acceptable but visibly weak or generic.
- 5 or below — material visual quality problem.

Do not inflate scores to validate previous work.

## Dimensions

### Character / recognizability
Does the interface have a distinct Spline identity rather than generic template language?

### Clarity
Can a visitor understand the offer and primary action almost immediately?

### Beauty / visual appeal
Is the page visually attractive, deliberate, and polished rather than merely correct?

### Visual lightness
Is there enough calm, negative space, and hierarchy, or do borders, labels, cards, shadows, and accents create constant noise?

### Composition
Are focal points, balance, scale, alignment, and spatial relationships intentionally controlled?

### Typography
Are type scale, hierarchy, line length, wrapping, weight, spacing, and technical typography handled well?

### Page rhythm
Do sections change scale/density/composition purposefully, or does the page repeat one pattern?

### Perceived quality
Does the page feel finished, credible, valuable, and professionally art-directed?

### Originality
Does it resist generic AI/SaaS/automotive clichés while remaining appropriate for the business?

### Commercial direction
Does visual emphasis keep `Find My Part / Request a Part` dominant and reduce time/attention wasted before request?

### Trust credibility
Does the page look credible without manufactured proof or unsupported claims?

### Mobile visual UX
At ~390px, does the art direction remain strong, readable, tappable, and calm rather than collapsing into stacked clutter?

## Target profile for Spline launch
- Character: >= 8.5
- Clarity: >= 9
- Beauty: >= 8.5
- Visual lightness: >= 8
- Composition: >= 8.5
- Typography: >= 8.5
- Page rhythm: >= 8.5
- Perceived quality: >= 8.5
- Originality: >= 8
- Commercial direction: >= 9
- Trust credibility: >= 8
- Mobile visual UX: >= 8.5

These targets do not override business judgment. A lower score can be accepted only with an explicit reason and no unresolved P0/P1 issue.

## Benchmark method
For each strong external reference, compare only transferable mechanisms:
- focal strength;
- scale contrast;
- whitespace control;
- typography;
- section rhythm;
- perceived finish;
- CTA dominance;
- mobile composition.

Do not copy category-inappropriate visual language.
