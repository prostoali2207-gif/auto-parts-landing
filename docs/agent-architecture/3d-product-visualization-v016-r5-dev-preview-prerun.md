## Decision target

Determine whether the remaining precision-interface panel read is removed by a genuinely recessed mating architecture.

## v0.1.6 changes

- cut a second, deeper keyed cavity into the cast housing;
- recess the open carrier from Y=-0.70 to Y=-0.40, inside the housing/front-interface depth;
- reduce carrier footprint;
- enlarge machined flange opening substantially and reduce outer flange area;
- reduce and offset service cap again;
- preserve the successful lofted housing, open structural bracket, mounting ears, ribs, connector, three-material budget and one exploded axis.

## Current visual reference mechanism

Fresh external visual research reinforces only the construction mechanism:
cast automotive housings concentrate identity in integrated ribs/bosses/mounting features and in recessed/machined interfaces, while open structural frames reduce mass and expose the actual mating architecture.

No real component geometry may be copied.

## Preview gate

Use 960×720 / 32 samples / render-only.

Proceed to formal full-quality R5 only if:
1. machined flange unmistakably reads as an open precision interface rather than a cover;
2. cavity depth remains visible behind the flange at the hero camera;
3. carrier reads recessed/supportive rather than as another face;
4. service cap remains subordinate;
5. housing remains dominant;
6. no new boolean/shading P1 appears.

If any of these fail, stop before full R5.

This preview cannot produce a professional PASS.
